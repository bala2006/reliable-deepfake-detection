Evidence status

Measured on FF++.
Not a universal reliability claim.

The local review service uses the configured production checkpoint and preserves an explicit REVIEW outcome. The retained headline metrics (ACC 0.6965 / AUC 0.8104 / ECE 0.2171 / region IoU 0.5576) are from 603 face samples in a report key named unseen_videos; that report sets video_level=false. Under the leakage-safe official-pair video protocol, the honest retained production AUC is 0.5592 on 142 videos. Raw Celeb-DF-v2 and DFDCP have no mask files in this extraction, so external localization is not established.

!

Operational boundary. These results support review-assistant use only. They do not establish zero-error detection, reliable performance on every generator, or a substitute for human verification.
