Research notes

Make the method
as inspectable as its outputs.

RL-ROI-Net v2 combines fixed uniform frame sampling, EfficientNet-B4 classification, and fixed landmark-defined ROI localization evidence. This page documents measured FF++ experiments and the limits that remain before broader performance claims.

Abstract

The detector processes fixed uniformly sampled aligned face frames. The deployed path uses a frozen honi05 EfficientNet-B4 backbone with a Focal-loss binary classifier and a dense region head, which reports fixed-ROI localization evidence for periocular, jawline, mouth, and hairline areas. Measured FF++ results exist, but external-dataset and genuine unseen-method evidence remain limited; REVIEW must remain a human-review outcome.

Research map

What the evaluation is designed to establish.

Q1

Feasibility

Can the supervised detector classify manipulated frames while producing useful dense forgery masks?

Q2

Localization

How well do predicted masks and fixed per-ROI outputs overlap the available FF++ supervision?

Q3

Transfer

How does a detector perform on held-out or cross-domain data when the backbone and preprocessing are disjoint from the evaluation source?

Q4

Reliability

How stable, calibrated, and equitable is the system across seeds, datasets, and subgroups?

Proposed protocol

Measure first. Compare carefully.

A credible evaluation needs data provenance, held-out examples, repeated training, and transparent reporting of classification, localization, calibration, and resource use.

See the retained evidence dashboard →
  1. Prepare dataUse the verified FF++ masks for localization. Celeb-DF-v2 and DFDCP have no mask files in this extraction, so external evaluation is classification-only.
  2. Train the detectorRun the supervised EfficientNet-B4 path in Docker on the RTX 4050 CUDA runtime, recording which backbone path is used.
  3. Compare baselinesUse matched uniform frame-level baselines and documented preprocessing.
  4. Report uncertaintyPublish seed variation, calibration, subgroup results, and measured runtime.
Limits & safeguards

Known unknowns are part of the result.

Bounded evidence

FF++ held-out results are measured, but cross-dataset generalization and genuine unseen-method performance remain limited. This tool must not be represented as broadly production-reliable.

Mask availability

FF++ supplies masks for its 5,200 fake records. Celeb-DF-v2 and DFDCP have no mask files in this extraction, so no external localization claim is supported.

Unproven robustness

Cross-domain generalization, compression robustness, and intentional evasion remain unevaluated or limited to the retained research protocols.

Human impact

False positives can discredit authentic media and false negatives can amplify harm; operational use requires human review.

Next steps

From implementation to measured result.

Phase 1

Prepare and verify data

Use the available FF++ masks and confirm the documented splits and face alignment; do not infer external masks that are absent from Celeb-DF-v2 and DFDCP.

Phase 2

Train and evaluate

Run Docker training and evaluation, then save classification, mask, ROI, calibration, and runtime reports.

Phase 3

Robustness & fairness

Evaluate held-out generators, compression levels, and demographic subgroups before discussing deployment.
