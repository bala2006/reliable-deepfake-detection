Why localization

Detection should show its evidence.

The supervised v2 detector reports a frame-level classification together with a dense forgery mask and fixed landmark-defined ROI localization. These boxes are review evidence, not causal attribution. Ground-truth mask supervision is available for FF++ only; external localization is not established.

01

Fixed, reproducible sampling

Every video uses the configured uniform frame schedule, making the evaluation path deterministic rather than dependent on a learned selection mechanism.

  • Fixed frame count
  • Uniform temporal coverage
  • Reproducible inputs
02

Classification + localization

EfficientNet-B4 produces a real/fake logit while the dense region head predicts the pixels and facial ROIs associated with forgery evidence.

  • Focal classification loss
  • Dense mask supervision
  • Per-ROI inspection trace
The workflow

Sample. Classify. Localize. Report.

  1. 01

    Sample uniformly

    A fixed number of frames is distributed across the clip.

  2. 02

    Align the face

    Each sampled frame is prepared as an aligned face crop.

  3. 03

    Predict both outputs

    EfficientNet-B4 classifies the crop and decodes a dense forgery mask.

  4. 04

    Review evidence

    Inspect the verdict, mask, and per-ROI localization trace.

Responsible research

Interesting does not mean deployment-ready.

RL-ROI-Net is a locally operated evidence-review tool. It has measured FF++ experiments, but no cross-dataset evidence sufficient to claim broad production reliability. Keep REVIEW outcomes in human review; do not use the output as sole proof of authenticity or manipulation.
